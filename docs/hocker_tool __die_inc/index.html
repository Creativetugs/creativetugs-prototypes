OUR HISTORY

A Manufacturing Tradition Since 1972

Founded by Ronald K. Hocker in February 1972, Hocker Tool & Die, Inc. was built on a straightforward philosophy: deliver quality products, serve customers well, and create a strong foundation for future success.

As the company grew, it moved to its current expanded facility on Webster Street in Dayton, Ohio, in 1979. Ronald's sons, Scott and Bill Hocker, continued the company's leadership and carried forward the high quality standards established by its founder.

Today, Hocker Tool & Die continues that tradition through its tooling, machining, and manufacturing capabilities, combining established craftsmanship with modern CNC equipment and CAD/CAM technology.
