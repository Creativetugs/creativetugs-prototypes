Full-Service Manufacturing in Newington, CT

Established in 1988, Allied Machining Co., Inc. is a woman-owned small business operating from a 10,000-square-foot facility in Newington, Connecticut. We deliver subcontract machining and complete assembly services for demanding applications across aerospace, defense, and commercial markets. From initial design engineering and rapid prototyping to full-scale machining and surface finishing, we provide the in-house capabilities required to bridge the gap from concept to production.
