Over 75 Years of Industrial Machining & Component Restoration

Metal Industries, Inc. is a full-service machine shop located in Hartford, CT. We specialize in bringing broken industrial machinery parts back to life, producing custom parts and prototypes, and applying protective engineered metal and ceramic coatings. Working with steel, aluminum, bronze, plastics, and composites, our expert team provides dependable solutions to minimize downtime for industrial, commercial, municipal, and agricultural clients.
