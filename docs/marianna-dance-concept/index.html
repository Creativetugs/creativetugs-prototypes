Guidance shaped

Led by principal and choreographer Marianna Vellopoulou, the school brings together more than two decades of teaching and performing experience.

Classes draw on the Royal Academy of Dance syllabus and Vaganova Russian technique, creating a thoughtful foundation for students as they develop their skills, musicality and confidence.
