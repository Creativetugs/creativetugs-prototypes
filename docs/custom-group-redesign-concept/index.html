OUR HISTORY

More than a machine shop.
A legacy of making.

From a small Woburn machine shop to a group supporting manufacturing and workforce development, our story has always been shaped by changing industry needs.

1969

Custom Machine is founded by Cosmo Pasciuto, beginning in a 5,000 sq. ft. facility in Woburn.

1978

Growth leads to a move to a 12,000 sq. ft. facility on Fowle Street.

1986

A move to Nashua Street provides a 25,000 sq. ft. facility and expanded manufacturing capabilities.

1994

The company reorganizes for a world-class environment and expands into aerospace and medical markets.

2008

The Center for Manufacturing Technology is established to help address the skilled machinist shortage.

2009

Custom Machine becomes Custom Group Inc., bringing together two member companies.

Rooted in American manufacturing since 1969.
