The First Bee Response story

Built from a Life of Answering the Call.

Founder Kevin McGuire brings 28 years in the fire rescue service to a coffee company grounded in service, dependability and community connection.

More than a name

“First Bee Response” is a promise: show up prepared, treat people with respect and leave the community stronger. That spirit guides our coffee, event appearances, vendor partnerships and race programme.

“I value the communities I have worked with and the opportunity to help others.”— Kevin McGuire, 28 years in the fire rescue service

Good to know

Frequently Asked Questions.

Quick answers about our coffee, mission, retail partnerships and event appearances.

What is First Response Coffee?

First Response Coffee is a gourmet coffee brand inspired by fire-rescue service, community and the people who answer the call.

What coffee and tea products are available?

Explore the current shop collection for signature coffee, flavoured and seasonal selections, K-Cup products and tea varieties.

How can I book RAT 9 for an event?

Contact the First Response Coffee team with your event date, location, event type and preferred appearance details to begin an enquiry.

Can my store become a First Response Coffee vendor?

Yes. Retailers can enquire about countertop displays, the RAT 9 retail display concept and mobile event activations.

Does First Response Coffee give back to first responders?

The brand states that it donates 3% back to fire departments and law enforcement as it grows. Contact the team for the current programme details.
