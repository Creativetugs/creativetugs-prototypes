FIRST RESPONSE COFFEE — HTML HOMEPAGE CONCEPT

Open index.html in a browser. Keep the assets folder beside index.html.

This is a front-end visual concept, not a connected WooCommerce build:
- Product cards and Add to Cart buttons are demonstration interactions.
- Newsletter form is a visual placeholder and needs an email marketing integration.
- Product/category links, legal links, contact details and booking forms should be connected to the client's final URLs.
- Prices and product details are reproduced from the supplied brief and should be checked against WooCommerce before launch.
- Product descriptions are concise concept copy; verify flavour, roast, size, ingredients and fulfilment details before publishing.

SEO foundations included:
- Descriptive page title and meta description
- Semantic section headings and accessible navigation
- Image alt text
- Basic Organization structured data
- FAQ content and internal section links

The concept uses the supplied logo and images. It does not replace the production theme or WooCommerce functionality.
