Over Six Decades of Precision Manufacturing Excellence

Founded in 1956, Winslow Automatics, Inc. is a precision contract manufacturer based in New Britain, Connecticut. We specialize in complex CNC milling, turning, and wire EDM, machining challenging materials like titanium, stainless steel, aluminum, and exotic alloys. From initial prototypes to high-volume production runs, our facility delivers dependable quality and strict dimensional accuracy.
