About Jo-Vek Tool & Die Manufacturing

Since 1952, Jo-Vek Tool & Die Manufacturing Co. has partnered with global clients to manufacture precision metal products. Based in Bristol, CT, our in-house engineers and toolmakers design and build custom progressive tooling, offering high-precision metal stamping, deep drawing, and CNC machining tailored to your exact specifications.
