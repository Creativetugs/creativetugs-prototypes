    The host

    Kev Kellie

    Morning host · Fast Lap Racing Network

    Kev Kellie runs WNFX Radio from Michigan. He does the weekday morning show and the trackside work for the Fast Lap Racing Network: race notes, driver conversations, and walkarounds at shows like Joker Reels and Wicked Rods. The station is one voice and a country music clock — built for listeners who want the hits and for local shops that want to be heard at the track.

      What is WNFX Radio?

      An online country station streaming from Fenton, Michigan, on Live365. The stream ID is a37821. The show that covers racing is the Fast Lap Racing Network.

      Who hosts the station?

      Kev Kellie. Weekday mornings are his show. The other hours are the country music clock on Live365, with his station IDs and sponsor spots. WNFX does not employ a roster of syndicated celebrity DJs.

      How do I listen?

      Press Listen live on this page, or open the station on Live365. The player at the bottom of the page keeps playing while you read the calendar.
