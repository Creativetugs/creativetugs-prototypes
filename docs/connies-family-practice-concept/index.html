Meet Connie Ash.

Family Nurse Practitioner · Blytheville, Arkansas

Connie is a Family Nurse Practitioner providing preventive and acute medical care to individuals of all ages. She brings a strong foundation in nursing education and clinical practice, along with a genuine commitment to her community.

As a rural healthcare professional and nursing educator, Connie believes in making a positive difference close to home. Her work reflects a simple belief: every person deserves to be treated with care, respect, and attention.
