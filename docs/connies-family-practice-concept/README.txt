The wellness journal section links to Connie's original posts and uses supplied/original
article imagery where available. The health content is presented as archived personal
writing, not current medical guidance. Review all health claims, sources, and images
before republishing on the practice website.

The healthcare photos are loaded from Unsplash and article images from their source sites;
an internet connection is required to display them.

This is a design concept, not a live or verified medical website. Confirm all services,
office hours, contact details, policies, and any appointment workflow with the practice
before publishing. No patient information is collected by this concept.
