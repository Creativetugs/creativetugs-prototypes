02 / THE AUTHOR
JANICE E. DALER · AUTHOR

THE PERSON BEHIND THE PAGES

A story that began with one person's journey.

Janice wrote Collision With Self, A Remedy from her own mental health journey and self-published the book in December 2014.

Today, she is taking the message beyond the page through a grassroots effort to place the book in the hands of people who may benefit from reading it.

Janice is in the process of launching a grassroots project to donate copies of Collision With Self, A Remedy to local centers of support.
