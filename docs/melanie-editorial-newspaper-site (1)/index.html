A LITTLE ABOUT MELANIE

Curious by nature.
A writer by habit.

Melanie McGee Bianchi is a journalist, fiction writer and editor based in Asheville, North Carolina.

Her work spans literary fiction, personal essays and journalism, with published writing in outlets including Oxford American, The Irish Times and The London Magazine. Her reporting and features have explored arts and culture, music, food, architecture and the people who give a place its character.

She is the author of The Ballad of Cherrystoke and Other Stories and co-creator of My Life, By Me, a guided memoir-writing workbook published with Chartwell Books.
