Case study

A guide clients can step through.

O'Connell Electric already publishes its services, markets, and projects at oconnellelectric.com. This concept puts the next questions in one place, in the same red, the same type, and the same offices.

The work

Construction, utility, EPC, service, and off-site construction.

The path

Planning, engineering, procurement, construction, and turnover.

The call

One inquiry, sent to the office that owns that work.

How this helps

A prospective client sees what O'Connell does, what it can put on a job, how a project moves, and who to call. The live site stays the source. This guide is the front door.

01 · The work

Choose the service.

Five ways O'Connell takes on work. Pick one. The example beside it is already on the company site.

How this helps

A client picks a service and sees a real project type with it.

02 · Capability

What can go on the job.

Crews, the kind of equipment the work calls for, and the offices that field it. Select an office to see the published address.

Line work

Transmission, distribution, underground cable, and storm restoration.

Substations

Substation construction and medium-voltage service.

Off-site

Prefabrication, material kitting, and Level 400 BIM.

How this helps

Equipment and reach sit next to a real office. A client in Syracuse, Buffalo, or the Albany region can see the desk that is closest to the work.

03 · The path

From first plan to turnover.

A project presentation. Each phase names what happens and what the client receives.

How this helps

The engagement process becomes a sequence a client can present inside their own company. Each step ends with a deliverable.

04 · How to start

Tell us the work.

The inquiry names the service. The guide returns the office and the phone already published for that work.

How this helps

The client leaves with a named office and a number. The inquiry can be reviewed and handed to the operations lead for that service.

05 · On the job

One place for the work in progress.

A sample job view. Updates, questions, and drawings each have a destination. This is a concept, using a project type O'Connell already publishes.

How this helps

During the job, the client knows where the last update is, where a question goes, and where the current drawings live.

06 · After the job

Service stays on the work.

Turnover is the start of preventive maintenance. The same guide keeps the service path open.

Test

Systems are tested and handed over with the record of what was built.

Maintain

Preventive maintenance stays with the crew that knows the installation.

Call

The service line remains 1-800-343-2176, reviewed at Victor.

How this helps

The relationship continues after go-live. A client who hired O'Connell for construction can start service without searching the site again.
