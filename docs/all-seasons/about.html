About the family

Pots, trees, and the same names on the schedule.

All Seasons is a family garden center at 5101 South Washington. Dieter and Georgia, daughters Jan and Jill, Jill’s husband Scott, and their sons Jacob, Adam, and Nicholas have been filling pots, running the nursery, and talking people out of the wrong plant for more than 45 years.

On the property

Greenhouses, a nursery, and a gift shop that smells like the month.

Walk in and the showroom is dressed for the season — candles, textiles, wreaths, and whatever the porch needs. The greenhouse stays in production all year. Out back, the tree farms grow what Dieter has already watched survive a winter.

  • HGTV named All Seasons a must-visit garden center for North Dakota, one from each state.
  • The downtown Grand Forks hanging baskets in summer 2025 were grown here.
  • Jobs start with an application at the counter. Call if you want one held.

Pepper & Peate

The shop cats. They explore, then they nap, usually late in the afternoon. One of them has been known to sleep through a fall display.

The cockatiels

Two birds keep warm in the greenhouse and whistle with Dieter while shoppers pass.

The koi

A pond inside the greenhouse holds koi and goldfish. Younger visitors are often the ones with the snack.

Hear Georgia

Wednesdays, 8–9am, on 1310 KNOX.

Georgia answers garden questions live. For an order, a delivery, or something in stock today, call the shop at (701) 746-7777.
