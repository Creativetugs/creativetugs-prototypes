Nursery & tree farm

Trees that already know this wind.

Shade and ornamental trees, evergreens, roses, shrubs, vines, and edibles — blueberries, grapes, raspberries, rhubarb. Dieter tests a variety on the farms before it is offered. What you take home is hardy here, and someone in the yard will help you choose it.

Deciduous & topiary

Shade trees and trained forms. Prairie Horizon alder is among the named varieties. Ask for height, spread, and fall color before you pick a hole.

Evergreens

Arborvitae, including Degroot’s Spire, plus the spruce and pine the windbreaks around here are built from. Care sheets are at the counter.

Fruit & edibles

Fruit trees for a yard that should feed you, plus small fruit. Varieties shift with the season.

Roses

Climbers and shrub roses suited to the zone. Above All, a salmon-orange climber, reaches 10–14 feet in zone 5. Roses carry a 30-day guarantee.

Shrubs & vines

Spirea, hydrangea, sumac, barberry, alpine currant, and vines. Bloom time and mature size are on the tags.

Tree moving

Two spades. The 55-inch moves trees up to a 4-inch caliper. The 90-inch handles 4 to 10 inches. Bring the form to the store or email it.
