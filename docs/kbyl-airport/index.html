A Community Airport With a Personal Welcome

Connecting pilots with southeastern Kentucky.

Williamsburg–Whitley County Airport is a public-use general aviation airport located approximately four miles north-northwest of Williamsburg.

We serve local and visiting pilots, aircraft owners, businesses, and the wider community with essential airport facilities and a welcoming place to stop.

Whether you’re arriving for fuel, arranging aircraft storage, or visiting the region, our aim is to make your time on the ground straightforward.

On-Field Facilities

Useful services. A smoother stop.

Practical amenities designed for based and transient pilots, aircraft owners, and regional visitors.

24-hour self-service fuel

Fuel available around the clock. Major credit cards are accepted. Contact the airport to confirm current fuel types and pricing.

100LL · JET A / A+ — CONFIRM AVAILABILITY

Hangars & aircraft storage

Hangar leasing and ramp tie-down options for based and transient aircraft. Contact management for current availability and rates.

ASK ABOUT HANGAR AVAILABILITY

Aircraft parking & tie-downs

Generous ramp parking and secure tie-down facilities to support both visiting and based general aviation aircraft.

CONTACT AIRPORT MANAGEMENT

Complimentary Wi-Fi

Free high-speed Wi-Fi is available to pilots and guests while at the airport for flight planning, weather briefings, or work.

AVAILABLE FOR PILOTS & GUESTS

Weather information

Access current local airfield weather observations anytime through the airport’s AWOS online service or VHF frequency.

Courtesy transportation

A courtesy vehicle may be available upon request for quick trips into Williamsburg. Contact the airport ahead of your visit to coordinate.

PLEASE ARRANGE IN ADVANCE

Additional support such as GPU / power cart, flight training, and catering arrangements should be confirmed directly with airport management before being advertised as available services.
