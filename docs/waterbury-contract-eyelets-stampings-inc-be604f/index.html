American Precision Stamping & Deep Draw Specialists

Located in Waterbury, CT, Waterbury Contract Eyelets & Stampings, Inc. delivers dependable domestic manufacturing across automotive, medical, military, aerospace, and industrial sectors. We manage the full process from design through complete production run, operating presses from 22 to 150 tons to maintain tolerances as tight as ±.0003". Working with a wide selection of materials—including carbon steel, brass, copper, aluminum, nickel, phosphor bronze, and silver—we produce precision components tailored to your exact specifications.
