About Atlas Metal Works, LLC

Based in South Windsor, CT, Atlas Metal Works, LLC provides custom metal fabrication solutions powered by modern workflow automation. Our direct AutoCAD and FastCAM file upload quoting engine bridges engineering design directly with production, allowing buyers to verify material parameters, part geometries, and manufacturing capabilities efficiently.
