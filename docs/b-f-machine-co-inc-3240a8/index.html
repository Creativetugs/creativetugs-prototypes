Five Decades of Mission-Critical Manufacturing

Founded in 1972, B&F Machine Co., Inc. is a family-owned and operated precision machining specialist based across two facilities in New Britain, Connecticut. As an ISO 9001 and AS9100 certified supplier, we deliver complex milled, turned, and fabricated components for demanding industries, including aerospace and defense, space, power generation, and nuclear sectors.
