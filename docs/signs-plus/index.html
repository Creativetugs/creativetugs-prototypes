What the shop takes on

One crew for the sign, the install, and the call-back.

  1. 01

    Manufacturing

    Signs of all sizes, shapes, and heights, built for the site they are going to. Cabinets, letters, and the smaller pieces a storefront still needs.

  2. 02

    Installation

    The same shop that builds the sign sets it. Jobs run from a single panel to tall roadside cabinets.

  3. 03

    Service and repair

    Lighting, refurbishment, and repair when a sign has been up long enough to need hands on it again. A public review notes one job finished within three hours.

  4. 04

    Yard signs, decals, banners

    Decorative signs, window decals, and banners for events, sales, and everyday storefronts.

  5. 05

    Large-format printing

    Wide graphics for banners and general signage, printed for the job rather than ordered from a catalog.

  6. 06

    Survey and permits

    Survey and permitting when the site needs them, so the job can move from a drawing to a legal install.

How a job moves

Call, look at the site, build it, put it up.

  1. Call or write

    Share the place, the sign type, and whether this is new, a replacement, or a repair.

  2. Site, survey, permit

    The shop looks at the location and handles survey and permitting when the job needs them.

  3. Build in Darlington

    Manufacturing happens at the Whites Circle shop, including banners, decals, and large-format prints.

  4. Install and return

    The crew sets the sign, then comes back for service, repair, or refurbishment later.

The shop

Family owned on Whites Circle since 1989.

Signs Plus Sign Systems, LLC manufactures, installs, and services signs, and stays on the job for repair and refurbishment. The company’s own description puts the work in one line: signs of all sizes, shapes, and heights.

Johnny Deas operates the Darlington shop. Angie Deas helped run the company and was the person many customers dealt with first. She died in 2021. The shop is still in the family.
