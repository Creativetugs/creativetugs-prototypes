01 / WHO WE ARE

More than five decades of precision, partnership and progress.

A manufacturing partner
that gets the details right.

Since 1964, DV Die Cutting has helped customers turn demanding designs into dependable components.

From our Danvers, Massachusetts facility, we serve electronic, industrial, medical, commercial and other specialized markets. Our capabilities extend beyond precision die cutting to include CNC machining, forming, laminating, screen printing and assemblies—helping simplify sourcing and keep projects moving.

QUALITY & COMPLIANCE
